# Product Report

Use this skill as final closure after setup/explore/plan/implement/test/verify phases.

## Objective
- Produce one canonical delivery summary for stakeholder decision-making.

## Steps
1. Consolidate scope requested vs scope delivered.
2. List concrete changes and impacted areas.
3. Include verification evidence and verdict(s).
4. Call out unresolved items, risks, and follow-ups.
5. Provide final status and recommended next action.

## Rules
- Focus on outcomes and evidence, not narrative.
- Explicitly state what was not delivered.
- Keep report concise and decision-ready.

## Output Contract
- Summary
- Delivered Scope
- Not Delivered / Deferred
- Validation Evidence (commands/tests/verdicts)
- Risks / Follow-ups
- Final Status: COMPLETE | PARTIAL | BLOCKED
