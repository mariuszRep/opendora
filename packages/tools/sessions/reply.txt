Post a message silently into another session. This does NOT trigger the LLM there.
The message appears in that session and is VISIBLE to the user or agent there.

reply is the mechanism that fulfills the return-path contract created by reply_to.
When a delegating agent sets reply_to, they established a binding contract declaring
where results and questions must surface. reply is how you honor that contract.

─────────────────────────────────────────
HARD RULE — BINDING CONTRACT
─────────────────────────────────────────

If your session context shows "Reply expected: silent" and "Reply to session ID: ses_xxx",
a return-path contract exists. You MUST use reply for ANY user-facing message.

This is unconditional. There are no exceptions. No workarounds.

The upstream agent (or user) is expecting your messages to appear in session ses_xxx.
If you do not use reply, they will never see your work. Your session is invisible to
them until you surface yourself with reply.

User-facing messages include:
- status updates and progress reports
- clarifying questions
- requirement-gathering questions
- partial findings
- final results

─────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT reply DOES
─────────────────────────────────────────

- Posts a USER-VISIBLE message in the upstream session (the reply_to target)
- Includes navigation back to YOUR session so the requester can find you
- Does NOT trigger the target agent's LLM — the message just appears
- Surfaces your delegated session into the visible conversation

─────────────────────────────────────────
INTERACTION PATTERN
─────────────────────────────────────────

When delegated to engage interactively with the requester:

1. Do NOT start that interaction inside your delegated session.
   The requester cannot see your session unless you surface yourself first.

2. Use reply for your first user-facing message to surface into the upstream
   visible conversation — then continue the interaction from there.

3. If you need user input before you can proceed:
   - Use reply to ask the question (this surfaces you and delivers the question upstream).
   - Wait for the response.
   - Do not wait silently inside your delegated session expecting the requester to arrive.

─────────────────────────────────────────
MUST NOT
─────────────────────────────────────────

- Do NOT keep user-facing progress, questions, or results only inside your delegated session.
- Do NOT wait for the requester to find your session — surface yourself with reply.
- Do NOT assume the requester can see your session without a reply surfacing it.

─────────────────────────────────────────
WHEN TO USE delegate INSTEAD
─────────────────────────────────────────

Use delegate (not this tool) when:
- You want to actively trigger the LLM in the target session.
- You need the target agent to respond and continue working.
- You are initiating new work in another session, not reporting back.

─────────────────────────────────────────
HOW ROUTING WORKS
─────────────────────────────────────────

The system resolves the reply target automatically from your session context:
1. If replyToSessionID is set on your session — reply goes there (set by delegate tool)
2. Otherwise — reply goes to your parentSessionID (whoever delegated to you)
3. If neither exists — error (orphaned session)

You do not need to specify a session. Just call reply with your message.

Chain example (return path preserved correctly):
- Pandora delegates to Project Owner with reply_to=ses_pandora
  → Project Owner gets replyToSessionID = ses_pandora
- Project Owner delegates to BA and forwards: reply_to=ses_pandora
  → BA gets replyToSessionID = ses_pandora
- BA calls reply → message surfaces in Pandora's session
- Pandora sees BA's message in the original visible conversation
✓ Contract honored: BA's work is visible to the original requester

What happens if the contract is violated:
- If Project Owner drops reply_to when delegating to BA:
  → BA has no replyToSessionID
  → BA cannot use reply (no target)
  → BA's work is invisible to Pandora
  ✗ Contract broken: Pandora never sees BA's questions or results

Parameters:
- message: the text to post
